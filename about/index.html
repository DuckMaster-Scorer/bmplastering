About Us

Professional Plastering Across Surrey

At BM Plastering, we provide professional plastering services across Surrey. We’re two highly experienced, friendly and reliable plasterers who take real pride in our work and always aim to leave every job with a smooth, high-quality finish.

We understand that having work carried out in your home can be disruptive, which is why we work cleanly, efficiently and with as little mess and inconvenience as possible. Attention to detail, reliability and customer satisfaction are at the heart of everything we do.

We take on all types of plastering projects, including new builds, extensions, full house re-skims, patching and repairs.

Whether it’s a small repair or a full property, we’re happy to discuss your requirements and provide a free, no-obligation quote.

Please don’t hesitate to get in touch — we’d be more than happy to help.
